# Squanchy Bakery — mock logger files, scenarios and test oracle

Generated by `generate.py` (seed `20260914`). Re-run with `python generate.py`.

## Conventions

- **Week:** 2026-09-14 00:00 to 2026-09-20 23:45 inclusive, 15-minute interval (672 slots in a complete file).
- **Timezone:** Asia/Jerusalem local time (IDT, UTC+3 in September). Files contain no timezone offset.
- **ISO week:** 2026-W38, which starts on Monday 14 September 2026.
- **Threshold:** food-safety limit is 5.0°C. Healthy fridges hover around 2.0–4.6°C with a small daily cycle and noise.
- **Raw files** contain only time and temperature. Some also have two or three device header lines (`Serial`, `Model`, `Units`). Branch and fridge are **not** in the file; they come from `loggers.csv`.
- **Excursion rule** (see `expected_excursions.json` for the computed list): an *alert* is two or more consecutive valid readings strictly above 5.0°C. A single isolated reading above 5.0°C is a *spike-ignored*. `ERR` skips and breaks a run. A gap longer than 15 minutes also breaks a run. Duration is `(end − start)` in minutes (first to last reading of the run), not until the first recovery reading.
- **Logger moves:** a reading belongs to the fridge the logger was assigned to at that timestamp. TL-0417 is the worked example.

## Choices worth knowing

- After TL-0417 was moved out of the Tel Aviv walk-in at the end of 16 Sep, a replacement logger **TL-0420** was clipped into that walk-in from 2026-09-17 00:00. Its file starts on that date. Without this, the walk-in would have a week of history and then go dark — realistic, but unhelpful for an inspector asking about that fridge after the 16th.
- Haifa TL-0231 is marked `unit=F` in the registry rather than auto-detected from values over 30. The file header also says `Units: F`. The generator stores the series in °C and converts on write, so 38.3°F / 39.0°F come out exact.
- One unhappy-path file, `TL-9999_2026-W38.csv`, is deliberately not in the registry.
- Twelve branches, all real Israeli cities, 2–4 fridges each. No freezers.
